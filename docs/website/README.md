# Website

Material for rebuilding codetrellis.dev.

- [`feature-atlas.md`](feature-atlas.md): every feature as of 0.1.18 for people, agents
  and pipelines, use cases from simple to advanced, headline lines, and the limits the
  site must not promise past ("Say carefully"). Keep it in step with what ships.
- [`video-research.md`](video-research.md): making motion-graphics video with code
  (HyperFrames, Remotion, Playwright footage), what the cloud container can render, and
  a proposed pilot.
- [`hero-video.md`](hero-video.md): the storyboard for the hero video: messaging, the
  acts, every beat with its footage source, and what to say carefully.
- [`section-videos.md`](section-videos.md): the sections below the hero and the short
  silent loop in each: what it shows, and which capture it is cut from.
- [`../../video/`](../../video/README.md): the video pipeline the research led to. It
  records the real app and phone and composes them into product video. The spike's
  four results and the workflow are in its README.

Public repo: nothing here may name customers or unreleased security findings.
